# Hand outline (not fed to the model)

Title: Пыль над Аресом. About 6,000 Russian words. Dark themes off.
Invented crew only. SpaceX is the company name on the hardware and the contract, not a character.

People: Илья Воронов, commander, 38. Марина Лебедева, flight engineer, 34. Артём Шилов, doctor and biologist, 41. Софья Ким, landing pilot, 29. Наталья Ершова, MCC shift lead, 45, on Earth. Олег Берестов, ballistics, 36, on Earth.

Plot spine: Ares-3, an early dust storm, a weeping seal on the greenhouse connector, a choice between staying and aborting. They print a clamp, drill a short core, and lift in a gap in the storm. The ending is quiet, not a triumph.

The pipeline is free to split these eight scenes across its own chapters. This list is the human reference.

## 1. Landing, forty metres of dust

POV: Софья Ким.
Goal: put the lander down inside the ellipse while the first dust already cuts the radar.
Conflict: the guidance wants a wave-off; Ким has one window before the storm thickens.
Beat: she calls the numbers out loud, Воронов confirms, the contact is harder than the brief.
Outcome: they are down, tilted a fraction, alive. The greenhouse connector is not yet deployed.

## 2. First night

POV: Илья Воронов.
Goal: keep the crew on the timeline without pretending the timeline survived the landing.
Conflict: the tilt puts a shear on the connector they have not mated. Лебедева wants to inspect before sleep. The checklist says sleep.
Beat: Воронов walks the cabin, hears the fans hunt, and chooses the inspection.
Outcome: they slip the schedule by one sleep cycle. Nobody calls it a failure yet.

## 3. The weeping seal

POV: Марина Лебедева.
Goal: mate the greenhouse connector and prove it holds.
Conflict: the seal weeps a fine mist on the lee side. It is not a burst. It is enough to kill the plants and, later, the air margin.
Beat: she names the failure in one sentence. Шилов says the plants can wait. She says the seal will not.
Outcome: the connector stays mated and is declared untrusted. The stay-or-abort argument starts here.

## 4. Storm charge

POV: Артём Шилов.
Goal: protect the medical and biology load if they abort at dawn.
Conflict: an abort saves the crew and ends the core sample the contract calls a success. Staying spends the seal's margin on a short drill.
Beat: he inventories what burns first in a dust strike and finds the drill power sitting on the same bus as the infirmary.
Outcome: he will give the drill one hour, not the planned six. He tells Воронов the hour is medical, not scientific.

## 5. The delayed loop

POV: Наталья Ершова, in mission control.
Goal: get a recommendation to the crew before their abort window closes.
Conflict: the loop is already twelve minutes late and getting worse. Берестов has a gap in the storm that is real and narrow. Management wants the contract language. Ершова wants one plain sentence.
Beat: she cuts the side channel, makes Берестов state the gap in minutes, and transmits only that, plus the seal photo Лебедева already sent.
Outcome: the crew hears a late, short call: a gap exists, the seal is their call, Earth will not pretend to be in the room.

## 6. Forty minutes of ascent

POV: Софья Ким.
Goal: compute whether the lander can lift in Берестов's gap with the mass they still carry.
Conflict: the safe ascent needs forty clear minutes. The gap is not a promise. Dropping the untrusted greenhouse buys minutes and ends the plants.
Beat: she runs the mass twice, once with the connector, once without, and shows both to the cabin.
Outcome: they will not lift at dawn. They will try to make the seal trustworthy enough to keep, or leave it and go in the gap. The choice is still open at the end of the scene.

## 7. Clamp and a short core

POV: Марина Лебедева, with Шилов on the drill.
Goal: print a clamp for the weeping seal and take a short core, not the contract core.
Conflict: the printer's first clamp warps. The drill's one hour includes the time she spends on the second clamp. Dust drums on the hull.
Beat: the second clamp seats. The weep stops to a dry salt line. Шилов drills until the hour, not until the science lead's original depth.
Outcome: they have a short core and a seal they will watch with their own eyes. It is less than the contract and enough to leave with.

## 8. Ascent log

POV: Илья Воронов.
Goal: lift in the gap and say plainly what they did not finish.
Conflict: the temptation is to call the core a success because they survived. The log does not allow that sentence.
Beat: Ким flies the gap. Ершова answers late and only confirms the track. Воронов writes that the greenhouse stayed, the core is short, and the seal is clamped, not cured.
Outcome: they are on the way home. Nobody cheers. The last line of the log is the dust still on the camera, and the fact that he does not wipe the sentence down to something braver.
